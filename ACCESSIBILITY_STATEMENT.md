# Accessibility Statement

**Last Updated**: October 19, 2025

## Our Commitment

We are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

## Conformance Status

This application aims to conform to the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/quickref/) Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities.

### Current Status: Partial Conformance

**Partial conformance** means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the accessibility standard.

## Accessibility Features

### Implemented Features

- ✅ **Keyboard Navigation**: All interactive elements are accessible via keyboard
- ✅ **Skip Navigation**: Skip-to-content link for screen readers
- ✅ **Focus Indicators**: Visible focus states on interactive elements
- ✅ **Alt Text**: Images include descriptive alternative text
- ✅ **Semantic HTML**: Proper use of HTML5 semantic elements
- ✅ **ARIA Labels**: Enhanced descriptions for screen readers
- ✅ **Form Labels**: All form inputs have associated labels
- ✅ **Error Messages**: Clear, descriptive error messages
- ✅ **Responsive Design**: Works on various screen sizes and devices
- ✅ **Dark Mode**: High contrast theme option available

### In Progress

- ⏳ **Color Contrast**: Some elements may not meet WCAG AA contrast ratios (#101)
- ⏳ **Screen Reader Optimization**: Ongoing improvements to ARIA attributes (#99)
- ⏳ **Keyboard Navigation**: Some complex components need enhancement (#97)
- ⏳ **Focus Management**: Modal and dropdown focus trapping (#98)

## Known Limitations

We are aware of the following accessibility limitations:

1. **Third-party Content**: Some embedded content from third-party services may not be fully accessible
2. **Complex Data Visualizations**: Charts and graphs may have limited screen reader support
3. **Video Content**: Not all video content has captions or transcripts
4. **PDF Documents**: Some downloadable PDFs may not be tagged for accessibility
